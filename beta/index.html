How to read the cloud evidence

Observed cloud base. Airport cloud-base heights come from KSBA METAR reports. Scattered, broken and overcast describe coverage there. Airport elevation is added to align the report with this chart. A clear report applies to the sensor’s coverage, not the whole region.

Near saturation. The cyan dew-point dot approaches the orange temperature square as the air gets closer to saturation. The halo is a moisture hint at that station, with no claim about cloud elsewhere. Sensor uncertainty matters near the threshold.

Estimated LCL. The dashed gold marker estimates where air lifted from VOR would reach saturation. It is a parcel estimate, not an observation of existing cloud. It can differ from the marine-layer cloud base.
